# THE GLOBAL WIRE - The World in One Day, Friday 20 November 2026

A wire-service monochrome front page: one picture carrying the whole of
Friday 20 November 2026 - 40 stories from 7 regions, every one dated that
Friday. Kyiv's hit bridges, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha, the Asian
AI sell-off and the UK-EU reset deal lead; 13 pictured stories sit over a
27-brief "Also on" wire, a ruled table gives the day in numbers, and a
pin map drawn from real country outlines shows where it all happened.
Every story carries its source beside it (Reuters, BBC, IMD, ICC, AP and
more), contested claims are attributed, and a red DATE CHECK box names
the Thursday 19 November stories deliberately left out. All artwork is
original drawings - flat geometric scenes composed in code, no photos.
The page is produced by draw.py, a deterministic Pillow program (Python
stdlib + PIL only, no network, no AI images) that renders entry.png at
1024x1024; main.py copies it to the output directory.
